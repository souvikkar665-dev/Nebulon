RESPONSIBLE SPACE DOMAIN AWARENESS

Open Science & Provenance Principles

PRINCIPLE 1 · OPEN SOURCES FIRST

Public Satellite Ephemeris

All orbit predictions and telemetry validations rely strictly on open-access scientific networks like CelesTrak, SatNOGS, and Space-Track public catalogs.

PRINCIPLE 2 · DETERMINISTIC ARBITER

Physics Over Black-Box Predictions

Machine learning is utilized solely for advisory ranking. Identity verification decisions require physical Doppler residual matching and human-in-the-loop signoff.

PRINCIPLE 3 · STRICT PROVENANCE

No Conflated Timestamps

Nebulon maintains separate audit records for physical observation time, source update, element epoch, and system retrieval time, ensuring complete data honesty.
